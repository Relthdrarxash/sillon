Plan, grow, and harvest your vegetable garden in harmony with nature and the seasons. Sillon is a 100% offline agronomic companion designed for beginner gardeners and permaculture enthusiasts alike.

KEY FEATURES

• Personalized agronomic calendar: indoor seed starting, direct sowing, transplanting, and harvesting dates calculated automatically from your region's last spring frost date.
• Living companion planting: instantly identify beneficial plant associations (growth stimulators, pest repellents) and avoid harmful allelopathic pairings between crops.
• Garden bed & square-foot planner: organize raised beds, track parcel occupancy, and optimize planting density effortlessly.
• Crop rotation engine: protect soil fertility and prevent soil-borne pests and diseases through a 4-year botanical family rotation cycle.
• Plant health doctor & organic remedies: observe symptoms on leaves or stems, diagnose common garden diseases (mildew, powdery mildew, blossom end rot, aphids), and treat them with natural remedies (nettle slurry, horsetail decoction, soft soap).
• Comprehensive vegetable encyclopedia: detailed guides for each plant covering germination temperatures, spacing, sunlight, watering needs, and cultivation tips.

YOUR DATA STAYS WITH YOU

• No account required, no ads, no trackers.
• The app does not request Internet permission: nothing ever leaves your device.
• All garden logs and plans remain stored locally on your phone.
• Free and open source (MPL-2.0): transparent, auditable, and community-friendly.
